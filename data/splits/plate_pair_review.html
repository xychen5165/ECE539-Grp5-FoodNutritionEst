Plate pair review

For each pair, decide whether both photos show the same physical plate: the food from the left photo is still present in the right one (food may be added). Use individual food pieces and marks on the plate; the plate's position can shift. Choices are not saved; record results in docs/development-log.md.

Ingredient rule: at most 120 seconds apart, every earlier ingredient present with at least its weight (tolerance 5% or 1 g), total mass not dropping by more than 5% or 1 g. The splits use this rule combined with the calorie-lens plate groups; each pair shows the combined result. Generated by src/html_plate_review.py (seed 0).
