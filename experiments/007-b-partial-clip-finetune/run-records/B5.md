## Outcome and interpretation

B5 scores were 0.3162, 0.3125 and 0.3220 (mean 0.3169, sample SD 0.0048),
versus B4 mean 0.3255. B5−B4 point deltas favored B5 for all three seeds, but
paired 95% intervals crossed zero for seeds 0 and 2; only seed 1 excluded zero.
Partial adaptation is the best numerical development condition so far, but it
does not demonstrate a stable improvement across seeds and does not by itself
meet the two-of-three-interval B7 gate. The protocol JSON says uncertainty on
seed 0; the frozen comparison script actually evaluates all three seeds, and
the report preserves all three without post-run selection. Full CLIP
fine-tuning, augmentation, loss changes and alternate crop margins remain
outside this controlled comparison.
