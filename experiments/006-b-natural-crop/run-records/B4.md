## Outcome and interpretation

B4 scores were 0.3254, 0.3275 and 0.3236 (mean 0.3255, sample SD 0.0020). B4−B0 paired 95% intervals were [−0.0333, −0.0029], [−0.0258, −0.0010] and [−0.0337, −0.0064], all favoring B4. B4 also improved numerically over B2 in every seed, but only seed 2's interval excluded zero. Energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat MAE generally improved versus B0; seed 1 energy was 0.274 kcal worse while other nutrients improved, and seed 2 mass was 0.510 g worse while all four nutrients improved. No gate, CLIP fine-tuning, loss change or target transformation was included, so the input representation is the intended difference.
