# Work plan

The team will assign owners using GitHub handles or member shorthand. Keep the full Canvas identity mapping in the private proposal.

## Course deadlines

All listed times are Central.

### November 1, 2026, 11:59 p.m. - Proposal

Submit a private PDF of at most three pages including references. Include the public repository link, architecture, training and evaluation plans, and member table. Owner: TBD.

### November 22, 2026, 11:59 p.m. - Progress

Submit a report of at most two pages excluding references. Show the current pipeline, loaded data, baseline results, an executed neural or integration attempt, and a dated development log. Owner: TBD.

### December 6, 2026 - Review ready

Prepare the runnable repository, results, analysis, and presentation slides. Owner: TBD.

### December 7 or 9, 2026 - Demo

Deliver the assigned three-minute presentation. The team's slot is pending. Owners: all members.

### December 9, 2026, 11:59 p.m. - Final

Submit the report and identify the repository version, reproducible results, and artifact access instructions. Owners: all members.

## Team checkpoints to schedule

Assign a primary owner and date to each checkpoint, accounting for its dependencies:

- **Data access and splits:** verify the subset, check image-label matching, and save split manifests.
- **First baseline:** produce a reproducible run record and metrics.
- **Neural stage attempt:** execute training/adaptation or integration and record diagnostics before Progress.
- **Integration and fallback decision:** run the connected pipeline, test the main risk, and decide whether to use the fallback.

Together, these assignments should cover data/evaluation, neural stages, integration, and experiments.

## Decisions before proposal submission

- Define the substantive components and training plan.
- Choose a justified baseline, controlled comparison, metrics, and partitions.
- Agree on the differentiating feature's scope and record remaining questions.
- Confirm compute, storage, time budget, main risk, test/decision date, and fallback.
- Confirm repository visibility/access and course AI policy.
